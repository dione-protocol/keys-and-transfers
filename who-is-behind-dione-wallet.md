DIONE Wallet is operated by Dione Wallet LLC, incorporated in Toronto, Ontario, in December 2024. That company is a daughter entity of Dione Protocol LLC, the Toronto company founded in late 2022 that builds Odyssey Chain. The entity structure is the verifiable part; individual biographies belong on the project's own site rather than in a support article.

## The structure

| Entity | Role | Based |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dione Protocol LLC | parent; the protocol and chain | Toronto, founded late 2022 |
| Dione Wallet LLC | daughter; operates the wallet | Toronto, incorporated December 2024 |
| Odyssey Chain | the blockchain | chain ID 153153 |

Two entities rather than one is ordinary in software: a product company separated from a protocol company limits what a problem in one does to the other. What it means for a user is that the wallet's contracts, support obligations and liability sit with the wallet entity, while the chain's parameters sit with the protocol.

## Who owns DIONE Wallet?

Dione Wallet LLC does. It is the operating entity named for the product, and it is the company to look for in a corporate register if you want to verify that the organisation behind the software exists.

Ownership of a company is a different question from ownership of your wallet, and the confusion is worth heading off: no company holds your keys. The wallet is self-custody, so your balance belongs to whoever holds the phrase - and if that is you, no entity can move it, freeze it or restore it.

That is also why support cannot recover a lost phrase. [Back Up a DIONE Wallet Seed Phrase Properly](https://github.com/dione-protocol/keys-and-transfers/blob/main/back-up-a-dione-wallet-seed-phrase.md) is not paperwork; it is the whole custody model.

## Where is DIONE Wallet based?

Toronto, Ontario, Canada, which is where both entities are registered. Jurisdiction matters for two practical reasons: corporate records are public there, and the legal framework the operator answers to is Canadian.

It does not determine where the software runs, which is on your machine, or where the chain runs, which is on its validators. A wallet's location is a legal fact rather than a technical one.

## Who is the team behind DIONE Protocol?

The verifiable answer is organisational: a parent company founded in Toronto in late 2022, a wallet subsidiary incorporated in December 2024, a chain operating since November 2024, and audits by a named firm on specific dates.

Individual names and biographies are published by the project itself, and that is the right place to read them - a support pack is a poor source for personnel, because names change and an article does not. What this article can tell you is what to check: does the person named on the site appear in the company's public record, and does the role they describe match what the entity does?

Anyone contacting you claiming to be from the team is not. The project's own policy is that it never messages users first, never runs phone support and never asks for a seed phrase. That policy exists because impersonation is the most common attack on users of any wallet.

## Is DIONE Wallet connected to DIONE ASSET?

No, and this is the disambiguation that costs people money.

DIONE Wallet is the Odyssey Chain wallet for DIONE, the chain's native asset. The name "DIONE" also appears in unrelated contexts - different companies, different tokens, different registers - and a firm with a similar name in a financial register is a separate organisation with its own history.

The way to tell them apart is the domain, not the name. Software comes from the project's own site, and the project's own site is the one the pack links to as the official source. [Spot a Fake DIONE Site or Support Scam](https://github.com/dione-protocol/keys-and-transfers/blob/main/spot-a-fake-dione-site.md) covers how lookalikes are built, and [Verify a Genuine DIONE Wallet Download](https://github.com/dione-protocol/keys-and-transfers/blob/main/verify-a-genuine-dione-wallet-download.md) the check before installing.

## Why the entity question matters less than it looks

It is tempting to treat "who is behind it" as the whole of due diligence. It is one input among several, and the more decisive ones are behavioural:

- **Does the software ship?** Releases are dated and repeatable evidence of work.
- **Does the chain run?** The explorer answers that in seconds.
- **Does the support route work?** [Get Help From DIONE Wallet Support](https://github.com/dione-protocol/keys-and-transfers/blob/main/get-help-from-dione-wallet-support.md) is where that gets tested.
- **Do the claims hold up?** An entity you can find in a register is a claim that holds up.

A well-registered company can still ship bad software, and a small team can ship good software. The register establishes that someone is accountable; it does not establish that the product is good.

## Checking an entity claim yourself

Three steps, and none requires special access:

1. Note the exact legal name from the site - "Dione Wallet LLC" and "Dione Protocol LLC".
2. Search the relevant public corporate register for that name and jurisdiction.
3. Compare the incorporation details with what the site states.

A mismatch is worth asking about. An exact match does not prove much beyond existence, but it does mean the organisation is not hiding.
