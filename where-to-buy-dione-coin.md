DIONE is listed on several centralised exchanges - MEXC, Gate.io, BitMart, LBank, Bybit, Crypto.com and CoinDCX among those the project names - and it trades on Ethereum as an ERC-20 token on decentralised venues. Buying on a centralised exchange gives you the token on that exchange; what you receive depends on which venue and which chain.

## The two kinds of venue

**Centralised exchanges** hold your balance on their own ledger, require an account and usually identity verification, and let you buy with a card, a bank transfer or another crypto. Withdrawal to your own wallet is a separate step.

**Decentralised exchanges** let you swap from a self-custody wallet with no account and no verification. You need a wallet, gas on the relevant chain, and the right token contract.

Neither is inherently safer. The centralised route outsources custody and compliance; the decentralised route outsources nothing to anyone, including the responsibility of getting the contract address right.

## The exchanges the project names

MEXC, Gate.io, BitMart, LBank, Bybit, Crypto.com and CoinDCX are the venues listed in the project's material, with CoinDCX notable for an INR pair.

Two caveats belong with any list. **Listings change** - venues add and remove pairs without notice, so confirm the pair is live before funding an account. And **a listing is not an endorsement**: an exchange listing says a venue will trade something, not that the asset is suitable for you.

[Is DIONE Available on Coinbase?](https://github.com/dione-protocol/keys-and-transfers/blob/main/is-dione-available-on-coinbase.md) answers the Coinbase question specifically, since it comes up constantly and the answer is short.

## Buying, step by step

1. Decide where the DIONE will live: on the exchange, or in your own wallet.
2. If it is going to your own wallet, create one and back up the phrase first - [Create a New DIONE Wallet: First Run Walkthrough](https://github.com/dione-protocol/setup-and-troubleshooting/blob/main/create-a-new-dione-wallet.md) covers the order of operations.
3. Open an account at a venue that lists DIONE and complete whatever verification it requires.
4. Fund the account, buy, and check the fee and the spread before confirming.
5. Withdraw to your own address if that was the plan, and confirm the network and the address character by character.
6. Check the arrival on the explorer rather than trusting the exchange's screen.

Step five is where the losses happen. A withdrawal sent on the wrong network is not recoverable by the exchange, and addresses that look alike across chains are the reason.

## Which token are you actually buying?

This is the part worth slowing down for. DIONE exists in more than one form:

- **Native DIONE** on Odyssey Chain, chain ID 153153.
- **ERC-20 DIONE** on Ethereum, the pre-migration token.
- **Wrapped forms** produced by bridges.

Buying on a centralised exchange usually gives you the exchange's record of native DIONE, withdrawable to Odyssey Chain. Buying on a decentralised Ethereum venue gives you the ERC-20 token, which is convertible but is not the same asset in the same place.

Sending one form to an address expecting another is the single most common costly mistake in this ecosystem. [Migrate DIONE From ERC-20 to Odyssey Chain](https://github.com/dione-protocol/keys-and-transfers/blob/main/migrate-dione-from-erc20-to-odyssey.md) explains the conversion, and [DIONE Migration Verifier Shows No Balance](https://github.com/dione-protocol/keys-and-transfers/blob/main/dione-migration-verifier-shows-no-balance.md) the check when a balance does not appear.

## Where to store it

For anything beyond a trading balance, self-custody: DIONE Wallet, or the same keys paired to a hardware device - [Connect a Ledger to DIONE Wallet](https://github.com/dione-protocol/setup-and-troubleshooting/blob/main/connect-a-ledger-to-dione-wallet.md), [Pair a Trezor With DIONE Wallet](https://github.com/dione-protocol/setup-and-troubleshooting/blob/main/pair-trezor-with-dione-wallet.md) or [Use Tangem With DIONE Wallet](https://github.com/dione-protocol/setup-and-troubleshooting/blob/main/use-tangem-with-dione-wallet.md).

The trade-off is the usual one. An exchange balance is convenient and requires trusting the exchange; a self-custody balance requires a backup you control and cannot be frozen by a third party.

A common arrangement is both: a small balance on an exchange for trading, the rest in a wallet. That is a risk decision rather than a technical one, and it should be made deliberately.

## Fees, spreads and the real price

The price you pay is not the price on a chart. It is the venue's quote, which includes the spread and the fee, and on a thin pair the difference is larger than any trading fee.

Before buying, read the quoted rate and the fee separately, and compare with a second venue if the amount matters. On a card purchase, expect the card processor's fee on top.

[DIONE Coin Price and All-Time High: What the Record Shows](https://github.com/dione-protocol/keys-and-transfers/blob/main/dione-coin-price-and-all-time-high.md) explains why quoted prices differ between venues, and why a chart and a swap can disagree.

## What buying does not decide

It does not decide whether the asset suits you, and nothing here is investment advice. It does not give you staking rewards - those come from delegating, which is a separate action after the tokens are in your own wallet.

And it does not make the tokens yours until they are in an address you control. That is the whole argument for withdrawing, and the whole reason step five above matters.

## FAQ

### Where can I buy DIONE coin?

Centralised venues the project names include MEXC, Gate.io, BitMart, LBank, Bybit, Crypto.com and CoinDCX, plus ERC-20 venues on Ethereum. Confirm the pair is live before funding.

### Is DIONE coin on Coinbase?

Not on the project's list of venues. Check Coinbase directly rather than assuming, and do not confuse a tracking-site listing with an exchange listing.

### Where can I store DIONE coin?

In DIONE Wallet, or with the same keys on a supported hardware device. Exchange balances are custody, not storage you control.

### Which exchanges list DIONE?

The seven named above are the ones in the project's material. Listings change, so verify on the exchange itself.

### Can I buy DIONE with a bank card?

On venues that support card purchases, usually with a processor fee. The wallet itself is not a purchase channel.
